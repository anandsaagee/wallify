## 4. Product-Level Long-Tail Keyword Generation Rules

### Pattern:
- **Title Tag (50-60 Chars):** `[Poster Subject] Poster – [Size] Wall Art | WallifyStore`
- **Meta Description (140-160 Chars):** `Buy [Poster Subject] [Category] wall poster online in India. Printed on thick 300 GSM matte art paper with fade-resistant inks. Fast delivery. Order now!`
- **Primary Keyword:** `[poster subject] poster` or `[subject] wall poster`
- **Secondary Long-Tail Keywords:**
  - `[subject] wall art print [size]`
  - `buy [subject] poster online`
  - `[subject] poster for bedroom / study / office`
  - `[subject] framed poster price`
